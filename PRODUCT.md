# Umbra Product Context

## Register

product

## Users

People using a screenless hydration wristband during everyday life and nights out. The phone app is optional; the band's core reminders work standalone.

## Product Purpose

Help users remember water through passive haptics, with an optional app for intervals, real biometric readings, tracked nights, and one morning check-in. Source requirements live in `side_notes.md`.

## Brand Personality

Calm, premium, restrained. The client reference is Oura meets nightlife.

## Anti-references

Avoid medical dashboards, fitness-data overload, preachy copy, and party-themed decoration. GSR is excluded from v1.

## Design Principles

- Keep the core loop passive: band buzzes, user drinks water.
- Preserve standalone operation when the phone disconnects.
- Show real readings and clearly explain missing or stale data.
- Keep one morning prompt, without follow-up notifications.
- Reuse the existing design system documented in `CLAUDE.md`.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow the project's contrast, minimum 44px tap-target, semantic-label, and reduced-motion requirements. Arabic/English and RTL are required for the intended Dubai release; localization remains pending.
